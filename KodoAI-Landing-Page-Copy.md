Buying another disconnected tool rarely fixes that.

The result is not automation for its own sake. It is a faster, more reliable process that your team can inspect and control.

## Featured solution: legal intake

**Section label:** Legal Operations

## High-Volume Lead Routing and Governed Intake

When a valuable inquiry arrives, speed matters. So do conflict procedures, qualification standards and the firm's authority to accept the matter.

Most intake stacks solve one part of the journey. The receptionist captures the call. A form collects facts. The practice-management system stores the record. Staff still have to reconcile names, search conflict sources, interpret routing rules, chase missing information and move the prospect toward an engagement decision.

KodoAI builds the integration and decision-governance layer between the firm's AI receptionist, practice-management system, conflict records and engagement workflow.

For standard inquiries, the system can prepare a matter for conflict-ready review in as little as 10 minutes. Engagement remains subject to the firm's conflict procedures and human or attorney approval.

**Primary CTA:** Audit Our Intake Flow

**Secondary CTA:** See How the Layer Works

### What the system can coordinate

#### Minimal pre-conflict capture

Bring approved call, form, chat and referral inputs into one prospect record. Capture safe-contact preferences and only the identity, adverse-party and matter details needed for the firm's preliminary conflict procedure. Preserve the source, consent status and follow-up history without inviting a full sensitive narrative too early.

#### Conflict-ready preparation

Extract and normalise prospective-client names, adverse parties, aliases, employers, related entities and other matter-specific identifiers. Search the sources the firm authorises, return possible matches with source evidence, and send uncertain results to the designated reviewer.

This supports preliminary conflict review. It does not autonomously declare a conflict cleared.

#### Adaptive qualification after the checkpoint

Once the firm's preliminary conflict state allows intake to continue, ask the next relevant question based on practice area, jurisdiction, incident, deadline, representation status and the firm's acceptance criteria. Flag missing, contradictory or urgent answers for staff review.

#### Deterministic routing

Apply the firm's approved rules for case type, venue, limitation risk, damages, language, attorney capacity, schedule, referral source and exception handling. AI can interpret the inquiry. The routing decision follows controlled logic.

#### Approval-gated engagement

Move qualified matters to the right reviewer. Trigger booking, e-signature, payment or follow-up only after the required approval state is recorded.

#### Audit and exception handling

Log the inputs, rules, possible matches, overrides and downstream writes. If a source is unavailable or a required field is missing, the workflow stops safely and alerts the right person instead of silently continuing.

### The orchestration layer

| Stage                | What happens                                                                   | Control point                                      |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Minimal capture      | Approved channels create or update one prospect record                         | Safe contact, consent, source and duplicate checks |
| Conflict preparation | Authorised records are searched using normalised parties and entities          | Possible matches shown with source evidence        |
| Qualification        | The system gathers deeper facts only after the preliminary checkpoint          | Required fields, urgency and uncertainty flags     |
| Routing              | Firm rules select a review queue, attorney or referral path                    | Deterministic logic and exception queue            |
| Decision             | The designated person reviews fit and conflict information                     | Human or attorney approval                         |
| Engagement           | Approved actions create the matter, issue documents, take payment or book time | Recorded approval before execution                 |

### Designed for firms where delay has a real cost

The strongest fit is a high-volume boutique firm with valuable inbound inquiries, several intake channels, multiple practice areas or jurisdictions, and rules that do not fit neatly inside a standard form.

#### Personal injury

Preserve PPC and LSA attribution, merge duplicate phone and form inquiries, escalate catastrophic matters, and route co-counsel or referral cases without losing the source or approval history.

#### Family law

Capture safe-contact preferences before follow-up. Match former names, spouses, children and related entities. Route possible conflicts, urgent hearings and domestic-violence concerns to the firm's designated reviewers.

#### Plaintiff employment

Resolve employer brands, payroll entities, staffing firms, PEOs and related companies. Extract event dates and evidence gaps, flag deadline-sensitive matters, and prepare multi-claim inquiries for attorney review without treating an AI assessment as a legal conclusion.

This is usually not the right fit for a low-volume firm with one clean intake path and a practice-management setup that already handles its exceptions well.

## Governance by design

**Section label:** Control Where It Matters

## AI can interpret the work. It should not quietly own the risk.

We separate probabilistic tasks from controlled decisions. An AI model can extract names, summarise an inquiry or identify missing information. Deterministic rules decide which action is allowed next. High-impact actions wait for the right person.

### Approval gates

Define who can approve customer, financial, legal or operational actions before anything moves downstream.

### Least-privilege access

Give each service and user only the data and permissions required for its job.

### Traceable decisions

Record source data, system actions, rule versions, exceptions and human overrides for review.

### Safe failure paths

Stop, queue and alert when a required system is unavailable or the result is too uncertain to act on.

### Maintainable rules

Document, test and version the operational logic so it can change with the business instead of becoming hidden technical debt.

## Frequently asked questions

**Section label:** Questions, Answered

### What kind of work do you automate?

We focus on repeatable operational workflows with clear inputs, rules, handoffs and outcomes. Typical projects include intake, qualification, routing, document processing, research, internal tools, reporting and cross-system coordination.

### Is this an AI wrapper or custom software?

It is custom software built around the workflow. AI models may handle conversation, extraction or summarisation. The surrounding system handles permissions, integrations, business rules, approvals, logging, retries and monitoring.

### How is this different from an off-the-shelf tool or Zapier?

Off-the-shelf tools work well when the process is standard and each step fits a supported trigger. We are useful when the workflow crosses several systems, depends on firm-specific rules, contains material exceptions or needs stronger control over how decisions are made and recorded.

### Do you replace our current software?

Usually not. We prefer to connect the CRM, practice-management platform, phone system, database, payment service and other tools the team already relies on. We only recommend replacing a system when integration limits make the outcome unreliable or uneconomic.

### Can the legal intake system clear conflicts automatically?

No. It can collect and normalise party information, search authorised sources, surface possible matches with evidence and prepare the matter for review. The firm's designated reviewer makes the conflict and engagement decision under the firm's own procedures.

### What does "conflict-ready in as little as 10 minutes" mean?

It means a standard inquiry can reach the reviewer with the required intake details, normalised parties, preliminary search results and routing context in one record. It is not a guaranteed processing time, a conflict-clearance opinion or an automatic acceptance of the matter. Complex inquiries and uncertain matches take longer and remain in review.

### How do you keep an agent from doing something it should not?

We define allowed actions, apply deterministic checks, restrict permissions and place approval gates before high-impact steps. We also log activity and create safe failure paths for unavailable systems, incomplete information and uncertain model output.

### Are your systems compliant?

We design for the client's stated security, privacy, retention and approval requirements, and we can work with the client's legal, compliance and IT advisers. Compliance depends on the firm's jurisdiction, policies, vendors and use of the system. KodoAI does not replace the firm's legal or professional-responsibility advice.

### What does a project cost?

Cost depends on workflow depth, number of integrations, data sensitivity and exception logic. We scope the smallest build that can prove the business case, then price production work and ongoing support against that scope. The audit makes the investment and expected payback visible before development begins.

### How long does a build take?

A focused pilot can move quickly when the workflow owner, access and test data are available. A multi-system production build takes longer because rules, permissions, edge cases and failure paths have to be tested. We set milestones after the audit rather than offering a universal timeline.

### What happens after launch?

We monitor failures, maintain integrations, review exception patterns and update rules as the process changes. The system should improve with operating evidence instead of becoming an unattended automation nobody trusts.

### Who is a good fit?

A good client has a costly workflow, a clear process owner, enough volume to measure the result and a team willing to document its rules. If a standard product can solve the problem cleanly, we will recommend that first.

### Do you work only with law firms?

No. KodoAI builds automation and orchestration systems across industries. Legal intake is a specialised capability within the broader practice, shaped by our experience building legal operations software and approval-gated systems.
